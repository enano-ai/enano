Our story
Calder Peake was established in 2009 by Gordon Calder and Iain Peake, who had previously run institutional equity desks at two
UK banks.
The firm manages three funds spanning global equity, absolute return and emerging markets credit, and is preparing a direct
lending capability for launch in late 2026.
The firm employs 29 people, of whom 12 are investment professionals, and manages GBP 2.6bn as at 30 June 2026.

Investment approach
Global Equity: a concentrated, high-conviction book of 40 to 50 names with an active share above 75%.
Absolute Return: rates and market-neutral equity sleeves engineered to a 4% volatility budget.
Emerging Markets Credit: hard-currency sovereign and corporate debt, benchmark-aware but conviction-weighted.

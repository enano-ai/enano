# Careloop pre-seed deck (fictional startup, demo data only)

## The problem

Home care agencies still run on paper schedules, group texts and spreadsheets. When a caregiver misses a visit, nobody knows for hours. When a state surveyor or Medicaid auditor asks for visit evidence, schedulers print six months of timesheets and highlight them by hand.

We have spoken to 41 agencies. 38 of them told us the same thing: the schedule is rebuilt in Excel every Sunday night, and nobody enjoys it.

## The solution

Careloop is an offline-first scheduling and visit-verification app for home care agencies.

- Schedules that rebuild themselves when someone calls in sick
- Visit logging that works in the caregiver's pocket, with or without signal
- Electronic visit verification (EVV) that clears the 21st Century Cures Act mandate without extra hardware
- Automatic timesheets, billing exports and a Medicaid-ready audit trail
- Family updates sent after each visit, without the scheduler lifting a finger

## Why now

- Caregiver wages are rising while Medicaid reimbursement rates stay flat, squeezing agency margins (we have seen data suggesting 3 to 5 point drops this year)
- The EVV mandate is now enforced in every state, and paper visit logs no longer pass audit
- Schedulers spend entire days on admin that software should own
- Post-pandemic, families expect real-time updates
